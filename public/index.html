Underlay is a Chrome extension. It changes how supported websites look in your own browser. You can set the background, colours, fonts and icons on sites such as ChatGPT, Claude, YouTube and Twitch.

Underlay does not provide the chats, videos or other content on those pages. It does not read your messages. It only changes the appearance on your computer.

Sign in with Google is used only to identify your account and connect the plan you bought. Underlay does not use Google APIs to create, edit or generate images. It is not used for AI-generated intimate imagery.
