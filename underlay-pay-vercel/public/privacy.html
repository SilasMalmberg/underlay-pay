Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 September 2026

This is the public privacy policy for the Underlay Chrome extension and the payment site. Controller: SF Studio, CVR 45802973, Havlykkevej 2, Denmark. Email: Malmbergsb@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Email and user id, if you create an account or sign in with Google, so the plan can follow that account.
  • Plan (Underlay, Pro, Max, or Play) and the platforms you picked.
  • Look settings in Chrome on your own computer: colours, fonts, glass, language, and backgrounds you upload. We do not receive your chats, watch history, or pins.
  • Payment at Stripe. Card numbers stay with Stripe. We keep the email Stripe has on the receipt and whether the plan is active.

What this product is

Underlay only changes how supported websites look in your own browser. We do not own or operate ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Meta AI, YouTube, Pinterest, or Twitch.

Promo codes

A promo code is checked on our server and tied to your account email. Clearing Chrome data does not reset it. Each code can be used a limited number of times.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 16.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your account data at Malmbergsb@gmail.com. You can also remove the extension to delete look settings stored in Chrome. You can complain to Datatilsynet in Denmark.
